# Code of Conduct

Markette follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, assume good intent, give and accept constructive feedback
gracefully, and focus on what is best for the community. Harassment, discrimination, personal
attacks, publishing others' private information, and sexualised language or imagery are not tolerated
anywhere in the project's spaces.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers, through a private message to an organization
owner on GitHub or the contact listed in the organization profile. Reports are handled confidentially.
Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and temporarily or permanently ban participants,
following the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
